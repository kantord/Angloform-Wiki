## Introduction

the science is a discipline. the science builds the knowledge and
organizes the knowledge. the science explains: the nature and the
society. the explanations of the science are testable.

the science follows a method. the method makes an observation and
produces a hypothesis. the method checks the hypothesis with the
evidence. the method produces a conclusion.

the community of the science challenges the knowledge and validates the
knowledge.

<details>
<summary>Source (English), paragraph 1</summary>

Science is a systematic discipline that builds and organises knowledge
in the form of testable explanations about nature and society. It is
driven by the scientific method: an empirical cycle that typically
involves making observations, producing hypotheses, testing them with
evidence, and drawing conclusions. Science encompasses this process and
the body of knowledge it produces, which the scientific community
continuously challenges, validates, and organises.
